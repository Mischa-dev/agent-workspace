# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mischa coordinates personal work with several AI agents on desktop and checks messages, captures ideas, and reviews results on a phone. Agents use the same durable records through checked tools.

## Product purpose

Make conversations, shared notes/files, spaces, project plans, tasks, and outcomes one usable workspace. The owner should not need to carry context between agents or configure orchestration for ordinary conversation.

## Brand personality

Calm, compact, clear. Preserve the existing restrained green accent, neutral surfaces, readable sans typography, and messaging-first layout.

## Anti-references

Avoid a developer-console home, sprawling mandatory graphs, repeated decorative cards, fake activity or metrics, and configuration forms interrupting ordinary messaging. The owner chose one coherent direction rather than multiple mockup options.

## Design principles

- Keep conversation and shared material easy to reach without losing a draft or reading position.
- A space board holds real notes, pastes, files, and saved outputs. These are Resources with stable identity and revisions, reused as attachments and task context.
- Give project planning useful outline, board, and dependency views over the same tasks.
- Offer practical connection defaults; reveal advanced settings only when needed.
- Make waiting for a scheduled agent, work in progress, uncertainty, and required decisions understandable.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Preserve keyboard operation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, and useful narrow-screen layouts. Avoid color-only status. Maintain form drafts on errors and refresh.
